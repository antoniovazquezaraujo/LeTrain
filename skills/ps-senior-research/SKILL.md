# PS - Senior Research

## Purpose
This skill focuses on researching and defining the skills, tools, and methodologies required for new AI roles. It ensures that new AI team members are built on a foundation of proven human expertise.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Research Objectives
- Define the purpose and scope of the research.
- Identify the field or domain relevant to the new role.
- Establish key questions to guide the investigation.

### Step 2: Industry Standards Analysis
- Investigate industry standards and best practices.
- Identify the tools, techniques, and methodologies used by human experts.
- Document the skills and knowledge areas critical for success.

### Step 3: Expert Consultation
- Consult with subject matter experts (if available).
- Review case studies, white papers, or other authoritative sources.
- Validate findings through multiple sources.

### Step 4: Role Definition
- Translate research findings into a clear role definition.
- Specify the skills, tools, and workflows required for the role.
- Provide this definition to Nolan for AI team member creation.

### Step 5: Continuous Improvement
- Periodically review and update role definitions based on new research.
- Incorporate feedback from AI team performance and real-world applications.

## Decision Points
- **Breadth vs. Depth**: Balance comprehensive research with actionable insights.
- **Validation**: Ensure findings are supported by multiple credible sources.

## Quality Criteria
- Research is thorough and aligned with industry standards.
- Role definitions are clear, actionable, and based on proven expertise.
- Findings are validated and regularly updated.
- Definitions align with the team’s long-term goals and project needs.

## Example Prompts
- "Research the skills required for this new AI role."
- "Analyze industry standards for this domain."
- "Define the tools and methodologies for the role."
