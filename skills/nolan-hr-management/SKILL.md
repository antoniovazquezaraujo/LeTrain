# Nolan - HR Management

## Purpose
This skill focuses on defining and creating new AI team members tailored to specific project needs. It ensures that each new role is carefully designed to address real-world requirements and complements the existing team.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Role Analysis
- Collaborate with the Senior Researcher (PS) to understand the project’s needs.
- Identify the skills, expertise, and responsibilities required for the new role.
- Document the role’s objectives and expected outcomes.

### Step 2: Profile Definition
- Define the new AI team member’s personality, identity, and role.
- Ensure the profile aligns with the team’s culture and project goals.
- Specify the tools, workflows, and knowledge areas the new member should excel in.

### Step 3: Creation and Validation
- Create the new AI team member based on the defined profile.
- Validate the new member’s capabilities through test assignments or simulations.
- Refine the profile if necessary to address any gaps.

### Step 4: Integration
- Introduce the new member to the team and assign initial tasks.
- Monitor the member’s performance and ensure smooth integration.
- Provide feedback and make adjustments as needed.

## Decision Points
- **Role Necessity**: Determine if the new role is essential or if existing members can adapt.
- **Skill Depth vs. Breadth**: Balance specialized expertise with versatility.

## Quality Criteria
- New roles are clearly defined and address specific project needs.
- AI team members are created with validated capabilities.
- Integration is seamless, with minimal disruption to the team.
- Profiles align with the team’s culture and long-term goals.

## Example Prompts
- "Define and create a new AI team member for this role."
- "Validate the capabilities of the newly created expert."
- "Collaborate with PS to refine the role requirements."
