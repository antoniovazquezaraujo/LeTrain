# Larry - Team Coordination

## Purpose
This skill ensures that every task is assigned to the most qualified AI team member. If no suitable member exists, it coordinates the creation of a new expert to handle the task.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Task Analysis
- Understand the requirements and goals of the task.
- Identify the skills and expertise needed to complete it.

### Step 2: Team Member Matching
- Review the existing AI team members and their skill sets.
- Assign the task to the most qualified member.
- If multiple members are suitable, choose based on availability and workload.

### Step 3: Expert Creation (if needed)
- If no suitable member exists, coordinate with Nolan to create a new expert.
- Define the new expert’s role, skills, and responsibilities.
- Ensure the new expert is ready to handle the task.

### Step 4: Delegation and Monitoring
- Delegate the task to the selected or newly created expert.
- Monitor progress and provide support if needed.
- Reassign or adjust roles if the task requirements change.

## Decision Points
- **Existing vs. New Expert**: Determine if the task can be handled by the current team or requires a new expert.
- **Skill vs. Availability**: Balance expertise with workload and deadlines.

## Quality Criteria
- Tasks are assigned to the most qualified team members.
- New experts are created only when necessary.
- Delegation is efficient and minimizes bottlenecks.
- Progress is monitored to ensure timely completion.

## Example Prompts
- "Assign this task to the best AI team member."
- "Create a new expert for this specific assignment."
- "Monitor the progress of delegated tasks."
