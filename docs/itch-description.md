LeTrain - Procedural Tycoon

LeTrain is a procedural train logistics and economy simulator that lets you play in two completely different dimensions — and now also record, undo and share everything you build.

Start your logistics empire in a procedurally generated infinite world. Lay down tracks, build stations, and manage a dynamic economy where you transport coal, gold, rubies, and more across vast distances.

✨ Two Games in One

The core logic of LeTrain is completely decoupled from its graphics engine, meaning you get two launchers with the game:

• LeTrain2D: A blazing fast, classic terminal UI built with Lanterna. Experience the matrix-like thrill of ASCII art logistics.

• LeTrain 3D: A modern 3D engine powered by LibGDX that renders the iconic ASCII characters as physical 3D objects in a smooth, orbiting camera environment.

🚂 Key Features

• Infinite Worlds: Custom layered Perlin noise guarantees a completely unique landscape every time you play.

• Deep Economy: Dynamic supply and demand. Connect resource extractors to cities and watch the world grow.

• Automation Language: A custom scripting language (built with ANTLR4) lets you write real code to automate your train routes, switches, semaphores and autopilot logic.

• Record, Undo & Redo: Hit Record mode and every edit is captured. Undo and redo deterministically in both the 2D and 3D clients — building, switches, semaphores and signals.

• Scenarios You Can Share: Export your whole network as a small `.ltr` text recipe and import anyone else's. The same seed rebuilds the same world, so a scenario travels as plain text — no savegame needed. Validate one instantly with the bundled `letrain-check`.

• Built-in Editor: Press `p` for a tabbed scenario/program editor with a per-tab quick reference and error navigation.

• Experiment Freely: Snapshot the live world, try anything, then restore it with a keypress.

• Open Source: LeTrain is completely free and open source.
